Everything a ride needs, on your Mac

Built for people who plan trips on a big screen before they go, and who miss BaseCamp's simplicity but not its age.

Routes that follow the roads

Click where you want to go and the route follows the roads as you build it. You can choose between up to three alternatives, then shape the route by dragging its line or its points. Every change can be undone.

Know the road before you ride it

One profile shows elevation, road class, posted speed limits and a twistiness score along the whole route. Where a limit is assumed rather than posted, the profile labels it as assumed.

Road trips, day by day

Plan a trip as dated stops lasting hours or days, and TrailRT plans the drives in between. A day-by-day view shows where you'll be and when.

Fuel, beds and food along the way

Find hotels, fuel, cafés and more along your route, and add them to the trip as stops. Place and UK postcode search work with no connection.

Your photos, where you took them

Link a trip's folder and its photos appear on the map where they were taken. TrailRT reads the folder and never moves, copies or changes your files.

Bring the files you have

Imports GPX, KML, KMZ, TCX, FIT and CSV. Exports GPX, KML, KMZ and CSV. Imported tracks are kept exactly as recorded, never quietly smoothed or resampled.

Download a region once. Route it anywhere, offline.

The routing engine is built into the app. In the UK, pick the counties you ride in from a map. Elsewhere, choose a published region that covers where you're going. TrailRT downloads OpenStreetMap data for those regions and builds the routing graph on your Mac. After that, routing works without a connection.

  • Several neighbouring regions combine into one routing graph
  • Terrain heights download with the region
  • OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap base maps
  • Send a destination to Waze when it's time to go

Good to know

What TrailRT doesn't do yet.

Apple silicon only
Needs an M1 Mac or later, running macOS 13.3 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.
UK first
The county picker and postcode search are UK-only. Routing and places work anywhere a region has been downloaded.
Garmin devices
Exchange routes with a Garmin device as GPX files. Sending directly to a device, and reading GDB and IMG files, aren't supported.
Map tiles need a connection
The base map is downloaded as you browse. Routing, profiles and search work offline once a region is downloaded.

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

TrailRT does not collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Your trips, routes, tracks, waypoints and settings are stored only on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you create or import stays in TrailRT's data folder on your Mac. When you link a photo folder, TrailRT reads the photos in place to show them on the map. It doesn't upload, copy or change them.

When TrailRT connects to the internet

TrailRT only goes online to fetch map data, and only when you ask for it. Each request goes straight from your Mac to the service named below. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and what was requested to that service, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Nothing about you or your trips is sent with these requests.

  • Base map tiles from OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap, whichever you choose, for the area you're viewing.
  • Map regions from Geofabrik, when you download a region.
  • Terrain heights from the Terrain Tiles dataset on Amazon Web Services, when you download a region.
  • UK postcode data from Ordnance Survey, when you turn on postcode search in Settings.
  • A routing server of your own, only if you set one up in Settings. TrailRT asks your permission first and names the server.

When you send a destination to Waze, TrailRT opens a Waze link containing that destination, and Waze's privacy policy applies from there.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It's hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard request logs as the host.

Children

TrailRT doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If TrailRT ever starts collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before the version that does so is released.

Contact

Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub with what you did, what you expected, and your macOS version.

Does TrailRT need an internet connection?

Only to download map regions and to show the base map as you browse. Routing, elevation profiles, place search and postcode search all work offline once their data is on your Mac.

Will it work with my Garmin?

Export your route as GPX and copy it to the device, or import it with Garmin's own tools. TrailRT doesn't send routes to a device directly.

Where is my data?

In TrailRT's data folder on your Mac, and nowhere else. TrailRT also keeps its own backups there.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no paid features. TrailRT is open source under the MIT licence.
